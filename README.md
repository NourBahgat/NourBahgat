## Hi, I'm Nour Bahgat

Final-year **Systems & Biomedical Engineering** student at **Cairo University**. I work where physiological signals, wearable devices and machine learning meet: I build systems that sense the body, process the signals, and turn them into something clinically useful.

**Interests:** biomedical signal processing · wearable and physiological monitoring · AI in healthcare · embedded systems for medical devices

### Research
- **Co-first author**, *Modalities and Algorithms for Generalized Motor Seizure Detection and Prediction: A Scoping Review*, **Expert Review of Medical Devices** (2026). [doi:10.1080/17434440.2026.2632757](https://doi.org/10.1080/17434440.2026.2632757)
- Systematic review of public non-EEG wearable seizure datasets, including FAIR data assessments (submitted)
- Graduation project: non-EEG seizure detection on a smartwatch, benchmarking signal modalities and algorithms on the Seizure Gauge and Open Seizure Database datasets
- Research intern, Nile University: 16S rRNA processing pipeline (DADA2) for an oral-microbiome meta-analysis in Parkinson's disease
